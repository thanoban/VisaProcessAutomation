# Pilot Discovery Plan

## Purpose

Use early pilot discovery to validate which pain points are truly worth automating first in the Sri Lanka process.

## Discovery rule

Public posts, social threads, and anecdotal complaints are useful hypothesis inputs, but they are not product truth. The pilot must validate them with real operators and applicants.

## Primary hypothesis areas

- ETA pending close to departure
- confusion about what proof is needed at airline check-in or on arrival
- unclear replacement or re-upload flow
- confusion between ETA, visa, extension, and appointment states
- rule or notice inconsistency across official channels
- extension and appointment friction

## Pilot participant groups

- Battaramulla visa officers
- airport immigration or port-clearance officers
- extension desk staff
- overseas mission staff
- help-desk or call-centre staff
- travel agents or airline check-in stakeholders
- inbound applicants from common source countries

## Discovery methods

- workflow shadowing
- short operator interviews
- applicant status comprehension checks
- document replacement journey review
- queue and case-log review

## Metrics to capture

- incomplete submission rate
- unreadable upload rate
- manual-referral rate
- ETA pending duration
- extension appointment wait
- applicant inquiry volume
- officer touch time
- override rate
- rule-publication correction rate
