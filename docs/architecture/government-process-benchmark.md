# Government Process Benchmark

## Benchmark objective

Use strong official government visa systems to improve Sri Lanka workflow design without importing their legal rules into the Sri Lanka domain model.

## Sri Lanka baseline

Sri Lanka’s current model is distinctive because it combines:

- ETA pre-arrival authorization
- officer clearance at the port of entry
- extension handling across online and appointment/manual paths
- public rule-publication inconsistency risk

That means VisaFlow must be more than a document-analysis pipeline.

## Canada

### What stands out

- personalized checklist
- post-submit document loops
- biometrics instruction and follow-up
- better status-tracker thinking
- passport-submission awareness after approval

### What VisaFlow should borrow

- checklist generation
- explicit next-step states
- document-request loops
- stronger applicant status transparency

## United Kingdom

### What stands out

- clearer appointment-centered application flow
- supporting document guidance
- translation expectations
- clearer timing expectations

### What VisaFlow should borrow

- document guidance by evidence type
- translation-awareness in checklisting
- cleaner expectation-setting

## Australia

### What stands out

- biometrics appointment handling
- app or centre path differences
- delay and “request more time” patterns
- operational closure awareness

### What VisaFlow should borrow

- exception handling
- appointment and contingency state modeling
- richer operational notices

## United States

### What stands out

- strong mandatory intake discipline
- post-specific interview handling
- fee and confirmation artifact awareness
- passport return awareness

### What VisaFlow should borrow

- stronger field completeness enforcement
- clearer proof and confirmation references
- cleaner post-decision logistics thinking

## Benchmark conclusion

Sri Lanka should remain the legal and process base. The foreign benchmark value is in stronger workflow design, clearer user guidance, better exception handling, and better operational transparency.
