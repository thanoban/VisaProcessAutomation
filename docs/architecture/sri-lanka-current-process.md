# Sri Lanka Current Process

## What the official surfaces currently show

This document summarizes the current Sri Lanka short-visit process model based on official sources reviewed during planning.

## 1. ETA is a short-visit authorization, not the whole journey

The official ETA overview describes ETA as an electronic authorization for a short visit and says the traveler must still produce the passport to the immigration officer at the port of entry to obtain clearance.

This means the system must separate:

- ETA or pre-arrival authorization
- port-of-entry clearance
- later in-country extension handling if needed

## 2. Sri Lanka short visits already have multiple tracks

The official short-visit category pages expose:

- Tourist
- Business
- Transit

The system should therefore not hard-code a single “tourist visa only forever” model.

## 3. Official public channels can disagree in timing and wording

As of **May 25, 2026**, the ETA site shows a 40-country free tourist ETA scheme and says foreign nationals are required to obtain ETA prior to arrival. At the same time, Sri Lanka Immigration’s general visa information pages still display wording that the **October 13, 2025 ETA-mandatory announcement was revoked until further notice**, while ETA services continue.

That is exactly the kind of publication mismatch the product must handle safely.

## 4. General eligibility conditions are still fairly classic

Sri Lanka’s published general conditions include:

- passport valid for at least 6 months from arrival
- adequate maintenance funds
- return or onward assurance
- acceptance of the stated travel purpose by immigration

## 5. Extension handling is operationally important

Official Sri Lanka pages expose:

- online tourist extension flows
- appointment-based handling
- head-office and manual handling
- fee and endorsement considerations

That means extension is not a side feature. It belongs in the state model.

## 6. Risk, misuse, and border-control concerns are visible in official materials

Sri Lanka official reporting and notices emphasize:

- misuse of visit visas
- fraudulent visa use
- border surveillance and fake-document detection

The system should support those concerns through auditability, exception handling, and better case preparation.
