# Tourist Visa Policy Chunks

## TOURIST-01-A
Tourist visa applicants must present a valid passport and the travel document should normally remain valid for at least six months beyond the intended period of travel.

## TOURIST-12-B
Tourist visa applicants should provide evidence of sufficient personal or sponsor-backed funds to support travel, accommodation, and return arrangements.

## TOURIST-20-C
Tourist visa applicants should provide intended itinerary evidence such as flight reservations, accommodation details, or equivalent travel planning material.
